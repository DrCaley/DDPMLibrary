# Configuration audit, 2026-09-02

Triggered by the spread-term finding: a shipped loss term that measurably hurt the
model and had never been ablated. This is a systematic sweep for the same class of
defect -- a shipped default that is wrong, unvalidated, or validated against
something that has since changed.

Everything below is measured, not inspected. Findings are ordered by consequence.

---

## 1. Every predictor's default uncertainty under-covered  (FIXED)

`mean, uncertainty = model.predict(...)` at the default, scored on the frozen
benchmark against a 90% target:

| model | what `uncertainty` is | coverage | correct factor exists in config | would give |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CorrDiff | calibrated by `CORRDIFF_SIGMA_SCALE = 1.6787` | **0.834** | `..._TIMED = 2.1801` | 0.908 |
| DistAttn | raw ensemble std | **0.737** | `DISTATTN_SIGMA_SCALE_TIMED = 1.621` | 0.899 |
| Stream | raw ensemble std | **0.489** | `STREAM_SIGMA_SCALE_TIMED = 3.304` | 0.895 |
| RePaint | raw ensemble std | -- | none fitted | -- |
| VCNN | zeros | -- | -- | -- |

Values as of 2026-09-02. Two have moved since: `STREAM_SIGMA_SCALE_TIMED` is now
**3.165** (the smoothing sigma was swept 0.8 -> 3.2, and the factor is coupled to it),
and RePaint has a fitted factor, **2.4513**. See `DEFAULTS_AND_DIALS.md`.

Two problems, both live:

**CorrDiff looks calibrated and is not.** Its default factor was fitted for
*simultaneous* observations. The documented use case is a vehicle collecting over
~2 h, where the correct factor is 2.1801. A caller asking for 90% intervals on the
realistic task gets 83.4%.

**Three predictors with identical signatures return three different semantics** for
the second value. A user comparing `uncertainty` across models is comparing a
calibrated sigma, two raw ensemble spreads, and zeros.

**Fix.** `CorrDiff`, `StreamDDPM` and `DistAttn` now calibrate by default, and the
factor is chosen from the observation timestamps (`ddpm_library/calibration.py`)
rather than assumed -- the tuples already carry the times, and the factor depends
on whether the field was sampled simultaneously or over a period. Measured after
the change: **0.904 / 0.912 / 0.898**. `calibrate=False` returns the raw spread,
`sigma_scale=` overrides, and `model._last_sigma_scale` records the value and the
reason. `RePaint` has no fitted factor and is unchanged.

Two integration faults this introduced, both caught and fixed: nine benchmark
scripts fit their own conformal factor from `predict()`'s sigma and would have
double-calibrated (they now pass `calibrate=False`), and a regex pass added the
argument to `RePaint.predict`, which does not accept it -- a guaranteed TypeError,
caught by an AST check over every call site.

## 2. Stream's sampler default was validated against superseded weights  (FIXED)

`STREAM_DPMPP_STEPS = 6`, commented as a validated "sweet spot", predates the
2026-09-02 direction weights. Re-swept over {1,2,3,4,5,6,10,16}:

| steps | RMSE | vort RMSE | vort corr | CRPS cal | width |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **0.08186** | 0.01819 | 0.528 | **0.0329** | 0.2150 |
| **2** | 0.08651 | **0.01654** | **0.571** | 0.0347 | **0.2160** |
| 6 (was shipped) | 0.09194 | 0.01743 | 0.562 | 0.0368 | 0.2297 |
| 16 | 0.09432 | -- | -- | -- | 0.2489 |

2 is the optimum and beats 6 on every metric, replicated on v1b, at a third of the
compute. **1 step lowers RMSE and CRPS further while degrading vorticity** -- the
metric bought by blurring. Changed to 2.

**A latent trap this exposed:** the conformal factor is coupled to the step count
(2.909 at 6 steps, 3.304 at 2) and nothing recorded that. Now documented in
`config.py`.

## 3. 38 grid cells carry a fabricated velocity  (NOT FIXED -- documented)

Traced end to end:

- the source `.mat` has valid `u` but NaN `v` at 38 cells
- `build_chrono_dataset.py` derives its land mask from `isnan(u[:,:,0])` alone, so
  it calls them ocean
- `nan_to_num` then writes **v = 0** there, in all 17,040 frames
- Stream and CorrDiff train on and predict at those cells (their masks have 3,787
  ocean cells); DistAttn's mask has 3,749 and excludes them

No reported number is affected -- the benchmark scores the 3,749-cell intersection,
which is exactly the set where both components are valid. But `predict()` returns
38 cells of unreliable values that a downstream planner would take at face value.
Fix on any dataset rebuild: mask on `isnan(u) | isnan(v)`.

## 4. Stale numbers in the docs  (FIXED)

- `PAPER_NUMBERS.md` still carried the pre-swap CorrDiff-vs-Stream gap (−0.0291).
  Recomputed at each configuration change rather than adjusted by hand.
- One result file lacked a benchmark md5 stamp; added. Every other
  `benchmark/results_*.pt` is stamped to a real benchmark.
- A CRPS convention slip caught during the swap: the v1 uncertainty table reports
  CorrDiff's CRPS on calibrated sigma and the others on raw, and Stream's raw value
  had been replaced with its calibrated one -- flattering Stream. Corrected.

## 5. Two curl operators in one benchmark suite  (FIXED)

`eval_vort.py` used `np.gradient`; three other scripts used an explicit central
difference. They agree exactly on the interior and differ by up to **0.043** on the
outer ring -- against a field vorticity RMS of 0.019 -- because `np.gradient` falls
back to a one-sided stencil there and `eval_vort.py` *scored* that ring.

Consolidated into `benchmark/_vorticity.py`; the outer ring is now masked
everywhere. **Consequence for the record:** the CorrDiff vorticity ablation in
`PAPER_NUMBERS.md` §3 was computed with the contaminated operator. Its conclusion
is unaffected (all three arms shared it) but its absolute values include boundary
artefacts and are not comparable with the Stream vorticity numbers.

## 6. Dead code removed

`repaint/loss_functions.py` (297 lines; one of eight functions referenced), plus
`training_loss` and `q_sample_from_prev` in `repaint/diffusion.py`. All training-only
code in an inference-only library, unreachable from any public entry point.
`distattn/diffusion.py` already omitted its equivalent deliberately, so this only
makes the two subpackages consistent.

## 7. Untested, flagged not fixed

These needed training runs. All are now resolved; the entries are kept for the
record of what was open and how it was settled.

- ~~DistAttn's curl/divergence term~~ **RESOLVED 2026-09-04: inert.** The first two
  gate-verified arms came out 1.1e-5 apart in weights -- 8 epochs has no power against
  142 epochs of the term already in the checkpoint -- so this was re-run properly: both
  arms warm-started from the pre-timecond base (epoch 136), whose trainer had no such
  term, 40 epochs each on one Titan Xp at the same seed, differing only in `lambda_cd`.
  The arms ended **1.5e-1 apart** in relative weight distance, 13,700x the original
  pair, and are **tied on all six metrics**; the control reached the better validation
  loss and divergence RMSE is nominally worse with the term. Combined with the 1000x
  dose degrading its own target, the term does nothing at any weight tested across
  three orders of magnitude. See `LOSS_TERM_ABLATIONS.md` §4.
- ~~The magnitude network's `λ_tv = 0.05`~~ **TESTED, INERT.** Adequately powered
  (the trainable logvar head diverged 4.5% between arms) and null on everything
  including the sigma roughness it exists to suppress.
  See `LOSS_TERM_ABLATIONS.md` §2.
- ~~`STREAM_UNC_SMOOTH_SIGMA = 0.8`~~ **RESOLVED.** Re-swept at the final defaults
  with the conformal factor re-fitted per value and the selection criterion fixed in
  advance (max r(sigma, error) subject to width no worse than 0.8's, at matched
  coverage). **Changed to 3.2**, factor to 3.165: intervals 0.2075 vs 0.2160
  (CI [-0.0090, -0.0080]) and calibrated CRPS 0.0344 vs 0.0347
  (CI [-0.00034, -0.00021]), both significant; r(sigma, error) tied. r peaks at 3.2
  and falls beyond, so this is an interior optimum.
- ~~`CORRDIFF_STEPS = 50`~~ **VALIDATED, unchanged.** Swept {8,16,32,50,80}: monotone
  the *opposite* way to Stream -- more steps better on every metric, and 80 buys 0.9%
  RMSE for 60% more compute. Useful control: Stream's stale default came from its
  weight change, not from careless tuning generally.
- ~~`DISTATTN_STRIDE = 10`~~ **VALIDATED, unchanged.** Swept {5,10,20,40}: stride 5
  buys 1.5% RMSE for double the compute and calibrated coverage is not matched across
  rows (0.880-0.924), so no setting is cleanly better.

## 8. Cross-device reproducibility (found while verifying, worth knowing)

`seed=` does **not** make results reproducible across GPU backends. `torch.manual_seed`
yields a different noise stream on MPS than on CUDA, so a diffusion draw differs even
with an identical seed. Measured MPS against CUDA on the same benchmark cases:
per-case fields differ by up to 0.035 (CorrDiff), 0.072 (DistAttn) and 0.224 (Stream)
m/s -- large against a ~0.1 m/s field.

Aggregates over the 40-case benchmark do converge: Stream's vorticity RMSE matched to
five decimals across backends. So **benchmark-level numbers are comparable between
machines; individual case fields are not.** Verified separately that the change is a
backend effect and not a code change -- with everything held on one device,
`calibrate=True` and `calibrate=False` give a bit-identical mean
(max difference 0.000e+00), and the calibration diff never references `mean_model`.

## The pattern worth naming

Four separate times in this work, a change that looked like a free win on RMSE was
buying it with blur: the ensemble mean over a single draw, a constant sigma over
the per-cell map, fewer sampler steps, and the magnitude collapse the anti-shrinkage
term exists to fight. Three of those would pass an RMSE-only review.

Only the structure metrics (vorticity RMSE and correlation) and calibration
(coverage at matched width) separate a genuine improvement from a smoother output.
The 1-step sampler result is the cleanest demonstration: RMSE and CRPS both improve
while vorticity correlation falls.

**Practical rule for this project: no configuration change is accepted on RMSE
alone.**
